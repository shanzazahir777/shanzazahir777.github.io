GOOGLE AI HACKATHON PAKISTAN

Dispatch

with Zain Syed

AI command system for emergency dispatchers

Dispatch is an AI command centre for emergency dispatchers. A caller reports an incident in any language or dialect, and a team of cooperating AI agents turns the call into a structured incident (sentiment, location, confidence score) and proposes a response plan for the dispatcher to approve or reject. The plan re-adjusts live for weather and blocked roads, and only citizens near the incident get alerted. It is aimed at fires and other emergencies where every second of delay costs lives.

Tech stack

Multi-agent orchestrationSeparate agents analyse the call, cross-check social posts to raise confidence, and plan the response
ExpoCross-platform app for the dispatcher and citizen alerts
Node.jsBackend that runs the agents and serves the apps
Google AntigravityAgentic IDE the project was built in

How it works

  2. Agents analyse it
  3. Dispatcher decides
  4. Right people alerted

Chemical Symbiosis

solo build

Turns one factory's waste into another's raw material

Chemical Symbiosis is an AI matchmaker for industrial waste. You describe a factory's byproduct in plain language, and it finds the factory best placed to use it as a raw material, explains the chemistry behind the match, and estimates the monthly saving in PKR. It targets a simple waste: factories dump valuable byproducts while the factory next door pays to import the same compounds.
